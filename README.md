# Life Insurance Pricing & Profitability Model

I built this project to develop my understanding of how life insurance pricing connects with profitability, experience analysis and financial reporting.

The model starts with an individual term life policy and then extends the analysis to a synthetic portfolio.

# What the model covers

- Term life premium calculation using mortality, lapse, expense and commission assumptions
- Annual cash-flow projections and profit testing
- Reinsurance and its impact on retained claims and profitability
- PVFP, new business margin (NBM) and break-even analysis
- Portfolio profitability across different customer and policy segments
- Mortality and lapse experience analysis
- Credibility-based assumption review
- Sensitivity testing for key pricing assumptions
- Illustrative IFRS 17 GMM calculations
- Unit-linked projection and an illustrative VFA extension

# Model structure

The workbook follows the pricing process from assumptions and mortality rates through premium calculation, projected cash flows, reinsurance and profit testing.

The analysis is then extended to a synthetic portfolio to look at premium adequacy, profitability and experience across different segments.

I also included simplified IFRS 17 GMM and VFA sections to explore how the projected insurance cash flows connect with financial reporting concepts.

# Data and assumptions

All policy and experience data used in the project is synthetic. The actuarial assumptions are illustrative and are not calibrated to a particular insurer or market.

The IFRS 17 sections are simplified illustrations intended to demonstrate the concepts and are not a full IFRS 17 valuation model.

# Using the workbook

Start with the **Pricing Calculator** for an individual policy. The **Results Summary** provides the main outputs, while the supporting sheets show the calculations and analysis in more detail.

The **Checks** sheet contains the main validation and reconciliation checks used throughout the model.
